Our story

A decade of listening to skin

Stackly began as a single consulting room with one belief: skin care should start with understanding, not a shelf of products. A dermatology-trained clinician and a skin therapist joined forces to bring analysis and treatment together.

Word spread through our clients. The room became a studio, the studio a team, and the approach stayed the same: study the skin, explain the plan, treat with care.

Before your visit

Questions we hear first

Still curious? Our care team replies within one working day.

Talk to us

We start by listening, then run a gentle digital skin analysis and talk through your goals and routine. You leave with a clear plan and realistic timelines. No treatment is done on day one unless you want it.

Arrive with a clean face and tell us about any medicines or active products you use. We may ask you to pause strong actives a few days before certain treatments.

Most clients describe warmth or a light tingle. For treatments that need extra comfort we explain the options first, and you can pause at any time.

Hydration treatments often show a glow the same day. Pigmentation and texture take longer, usually a few weeks of consistent care. We will give you an honest timeline for your skin.

Most facials have none. Peels can bring light flaking for a couple of days, and we will tell you exactly what to expect and how to care for your skin afterwards.
