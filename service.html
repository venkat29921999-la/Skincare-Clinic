Acne & skin care

Active acne and the marks it leaves need different treatments. We calm breakouts first, then treat scars once skin is stable.

✦✦✦✦✦

  • Medical peels

    Salicylic and mandelic acids clear pores and calm active breakouts. A course of 4 to 6 sessions is typical.

  • Light therapy

    Blue and red light reduce acne bacteria and inflammation without downtime.

  • Microneedling

    Stimulates collagen to soften atrophic (pitted) scars. Results build over several sessions.

  • Fractional laser

    For deeper, established scars. We assess scar type first so the settings match.

  • Prescription home care

    Retinoids and topicals, prescribed by our dermatologist, keep results in place.
